## Slide 2 — Problem
You tap "buy" on an app. The gold price ticks up on your screen.
It feels like your money is growing, safely, in real time.

Tag: THE APP SCREEN

## Slide 3 — Setup
The price you buy at and the price you can sell at
are not the same number.

Small tag: BUY ≠ SELL

## Slide 4 — Mechanism
Label: THE SPREAD

Every digital gold app quotes two prices for the same gram, at the same
moment: a buy price and a lower sell-back price. On top of that, 3% GST is
added when you buy, and it is never refunded when you sell.

Bar 1: BUY PRICE — full-width bar
Bar 2: SELL-BACK PRICE — shorter bar

## Slide 5 — Example
Tag: ₹10,000 IN

You buy ₹10,000 of digital gold today.
GST takes a slice before a single gram is credited to you.

Sell it back the same day, at the same market price,
and the spread takes another slice on the way out.

## Slide 6 — Reveal
The number on your screen is the price gold is trading at.

Not the price you'll actually get
if you sell it back tomorrow. (red emphasis)

## Slide 7 — Insight
This is not a scam. It is still real, stored gold.

Tag: THE UPSIDE (green)

It just isn't built for quick buying and selling.
It's built for holding, not trading. (green emphasis)
