## Slide 1 — Cover
Two mutual funds. Same market. Same twenty years.
One number made you ₹9.77 lakh richer.

Tag: THE EXPENSE RATIO TRAP

## Slide 2 — Problem
You checked the fund's past returns before investing.

You never checked what it charges you, every single year, to hold it.

Tag: THE FACTSHEET

## Slide 3 — Setup
Expense ratio is not a one-time fee.
It's cut from your money every year, quietly, before you see a return.

Small tag: FEE ≠ ONE-TIME COST

## Slide 4 — Mechanism
Label: THE MATH

A ₹10,000 monthly SIP, for 20 years, in the same market. Only the
expense ratio changes.

Bar 1: LOW FEE (0.5%) — full-width bar
Bar 2: HIGH FEE (1.5%) — shorter bar

## Slide 5 — Example
Tag: THE NUMBERS

₹24,00,000 invested over 20 years, same 12% market.

At a 0.5% expense ratio: ₹86.6 lakh.
At a 1.5% expense ratio: ₹76.8 lakh.

## Slide 6 — Reveal
Same market. Same fund category. Same 20 years.

The ₹9.77 lakh gap came from
a single number in small print (red emphasis)
not from stock picks or bad timing.

## Slide 7 — Insight
This is not an argument against active funds.

Tag: THE UPSIDE (green)

A lower expense ratio doesn't mean a worse fund.
It means less of your return is spent before it reaches you. (green emphasis)

## Slide 8 — Takeaway
Before you invest, do the math:

Tag: CHECK THIS ✓

01
Compare expense ratios before you compare past returns.

## Slide 9 — CTA
Stop picking funds by their last five years.
Start picking them by what they quietly take from your next twenty.
