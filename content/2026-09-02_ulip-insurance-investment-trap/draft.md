## Slide 1 — Cover
One plan. Insurance and investment.
It struggles to be good at either.

Tag: THE ULIP TRAP

## Slide 2 — Problem
An agent opens a brochure and points to one line.

"One premium, life cover
and market returns."

Tag: THE PITCH

## Slide 3 — Setup
A ULIP is not one product.
It's two products, stitched
into one premium.

Small tag: TWO IN ONE

## Slide 4 — Mechanism
Label: WHERE YOUR PREMIUM GOES

Before a rupee reaches the market, it passes through allocation
charges, admin charges, fund management charges and the cost of
your life cover.

Bar 1: PREMIUM PAID — full-width bar
Bar 2: ACTUALLY INVESTED — shorter bar

## Slide 5 — Example
Tag: PREMIUM

A ₹1,00,000 premium, before it becomes an investment.

Allocation, admin, fund management and mortality charges. Together,
they decide how much starts working.

## Slide 6 — Reveal
A ULIP rarely insures you as well as a plain term plan.

And it rarely grows your money
like a plain index fund (red emphasis)
not while paying for cover too.

## Slide 7 — Insight
There's a cost to leaving early too.

Tag: THE LOCK-IN (gold)

Most ULIPs hold your money for 5 years.
Exit early, lose part of it. (red emphasis)
