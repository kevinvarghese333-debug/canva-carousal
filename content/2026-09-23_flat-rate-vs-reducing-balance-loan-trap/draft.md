## Slide 4 — Mechanism
Background: navy. Bar/comparison, huge figures as visual objects.

Label: THE TWO METHODS

Reducing balance charges interest only on what you still owe.
Flat rate charges interest on the full loan, every month, till the end.

Bar 1: REDUCING BALANCE (SHRINKS EACH MONTH) — shrinking bar
Bar 2: FLAT RATE (STAYS FULL THE WHOLE TENURE) — full-width bar

## Slide 5 — Example
Background: off-white. Worked-example / rate-reveal slide.

Tag: THE REVEAL

A ₹3,00,000 loan, 3 years, quoted at a 12% flat rate.

Converted to the same basis as a normal bank loan, that 12% works out to
roughly 21 to 22% a year.

## Slide 6 — Reveal

## Slide 7 — Insight
Background: off-white.

This isn't illegal. It's just rarely explained.

Tag: THE UPSIDE (green)

You can ask which method applies, before you sign.
The bank has to tell you if you ask. (green emphasis)

## Slide 8 — Takeaway
Background: navy, gold warning/detail block.

Before you sign a loan agreement, ask three things:

Tag: THE CHECK ✓

01  Is this rate flat or reducing balance?
02  What is the effective reducing rate, in writing?
03  Compare that number, not the headline number.
